# Cash-funded options and commodity access — September 27, 2026

Research jurisdiction: Colorado, United States, as specified in the foundation.
Chris permits researching purchased options and fully covered option sales, covering
U.S. equity/ETF and crypto markets. He also wants commodity/futures access without
borrowing, exposure beyond available cash, or settlement barriers to repeated trading.
This updates the foundation's original research scope. No broker account, credential,
paid feed, live order, or derivatives simulator was enabled by this research.

## Finding

No single broker was verified to meet every condition. There are useful candidates,
including an IBKR documentation lead on same-day options balances that needs resolution.
Do not encode an assumed exemption from settlement or collateral requirements.

U.S. securities generally settle T+1. In a cash account, a position bought with settled
cash may be sold the same day; the constraint concerns reusing unsettled proceeds.
T+1 is not a requirement to hold every purchased position overnight.
[SEC settlement FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/exams/educationhelpguidesfaqs/t1-faq),
[Tradier cash-funds explanation](https://support.tradier.com/kb/guide/en/funds-available-to-trade-with-YYcVFd8oSx/Steps/5170359).

Futures margin is a performance deposit, not the same as a stock purchase loan.
Ordinary futures nevertheless require collateral and can create obligations beyond
the initial deposit. A stop or low intraday margin does not cap those obligations.
[CME margin explanation](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/courses/introduction-to-futures/margin-know-what-is-needed),
[NFA investor guidance](https://www.nfa.futures.org/investors/investor-resources/files/investor-best-practices.html).

## Commodity and crypto exposure

Fully paid, unleveraged commodity ETF shares or purchased options on eligible commodity
ETFs are a closer architectural match to the cash requirement than ordinary futures.
That is an implementation recommendation, not a claim of profitability; securities
settlement still applies. Physically backed and futures-based funds have different
exposures, expenses and tracking behavior and must not be treated as owning raw goods.

U.S.-listed Bitcoin ETF options are another crypto-exposure route: Nasdaq confirms
IBIT options were listed on November 19, 2024. They are securities options on ETF
shares, with exchange hours and securities-account rules, not 24/7 native-coin options.
[Nasdaq IBIT options announcement](https://www.nasdaq.com/newsroom/nasdaq-listed-ibit-options-end-first-day-top-1-all-options-products-traded).

Deribit lists the United States among restricted jurisdictions; it is not a suitable
account route for this US project. The old LedgerX/MIAXdx fully collateralized venue
cannot be assumed available from historical descriptions: MIAX announced its January
2026 sale to a Robinhood/Susquehanna venture, and the current Rothera site focuses on
prediction markets. Current vanilla crypto-option retail/API availability was not verified.
[Deribit restrictions](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944487427741-Restricted-Jurisdictions),
[MIAX January 2026 sale announcement](https://www.miaxglobal.com/sites/default/files/press_release-files/MIAX%20Press%20Release%2001212026.pdf),

## Local application design before any option paper orders

Keep a separate broker adapter and paper account. Store settled cash, unsettled cash
by settlement date, reserved premiums/fees, and committed underlying coverage. Admit
only explicit instrument types and permissioned strategies. Reject an order whose
premium plus fees exceeds eligible settled cash; never interpret displayed buying
power as permission to borrow.

Use each contract's actual multiplier and deliverable, including adjusted contracts.
Covered calls require unencumbered deliverable shares reserved across all open orders
and positions. Cash-secured puts would require full assignment cash and an explicit
strategy decision; their broker availability does not automatically authorize them.
Purchased options need exercise/expiry handling that cannot create unaffordable
underlying positions. Selling an owned option to close is distinct from writing an
uncovered option. Closing before expiry reduces some exposure but is not guaranteed
in an outage; broker exercise instructions and assignment terms still matter.

Persist synchronized option/underlying quotes, spreads, quote timestamps and contract
state. Model trading hours, expirations, exercise, assignment, settlement and costs
before claiming an options paper result. API-supported coverage controls and settlement
behavior must be verified for the actual account. The program can run locally while
using an authorized broker's outbound data/API connection.
