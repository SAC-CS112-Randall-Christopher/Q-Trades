## Prospective operating contract

One operator start freezes the policy. Up to twenty managed/reserved accounts
include originals, controls, experimental parents, trials and draining accounts.
An experiment and its equal-capital reference start together with matching costs,
warmup, risk, observations and rules except the declared mechanism/change. The
continuing parent is never the child's lifetime-equity comparison. Initial funds
are separately journaled hypothetical allocations, never replenishments.

Reviews occur once at the declared horizon. Fresh executable equity, full-window
coverage, matched reference, passive/cash comparisons and declared operating costs
are required for an economic conclusion. Missing data is blocked. A complete window
with no executed exposure in either account is low information; a supported executed
window without economic advantage is inconclusive. A promising result permits preservation and descendants,
not formal promotion. Failures and inconclusive outcomes remain retrievable.

Retirement stops and cancels new entries through the engine, continues exits, and
releases capacity only after flat reconciled financial closure. Uncertain orders,
reservations, faulted accounts, holdings and dust keep their slots. Archived account
projections and all existing journal records remain immutable and pageable.

Proposal generation pauses separately from new entries. Protection and both pauses
are durable. Daily creation/hourly work budgets use UTC policy periods persisted
across process restart. Active work and historical identity have separate bounds.
Physical storage/free-space gates pause research visibly and do not discard evidence.

## Operator workflow after a separately authorized installation

AI Lab opens the continuous panel. Inspect current activity and storage first.
Declare capital, operating cost, fixed review and holding presets plus allocation
and work/storage budgets; start once. The worker presents its next action or blocked
reason and the proposal inbox exposes its exact permitted inputs and evaluation.
Inspect a trial's frozen changes and account evidence before using its controls.
Pause proposals and new entries independently. Protection prevents automatic
retirement; unprotect an experimental account explicitly before safe retirement.
Exits and authoritative closure continue, and archived account/trial evidence is
available through bounded history and journal pages.

The proposal inbox distinguishes permanent rejection from a temporary wait and
retains each reason. Later eligible queued work proceeds while blocked work waits
for its recorded retry. A disclosed past outcome can train the proposer but cannot
serve as an untouched validation interval afterward. Independent admissions rotate
declared holding horizons through durable allocation, including after restart.

An external storage plan is frozen at worker startup; saving one does not restart
the worker. Its target identity must match the mounted local NTFS volume. A storage
outage pauses new trials and capture with visible omissions while position management
continues. The retained tier preserves exact history until capacity; export/capacity
decisions are explicit. Charts use local observations and cannot grant trading or
promotion authority.

Reopen the exact latest decision under Research storage to request an isolated
execution replay. Interval evaluators read verified v1/v2 dependencies with actual
availability metadata and frozen source hashes. Legacy v2 captures whose first-seen
availability was never recorded remain unavailable for causal interval evaluation;
their original packets and exact references remain inspectable. Missing or oversized
intervals produce unavailable/inconclusive receipts, never a substituted first-N set.
