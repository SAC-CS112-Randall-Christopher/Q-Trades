# Source-linked stock research

The AI Lab can resolve a current SEC ticker or explicit CIK, select official
reported US-GAAP facts known before a declared cutoff, calculate compatible
liabilities/assets, inspect two accession-linked filing excerpts, and save/reopen
a reproducible read-only study. This is equity research; no broker/equity-order
route was added. The price-only IBM study works independently of SEC access.

Current source policy was checked October 1, 2026 against the official
[SEC developer guidance](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources),
[SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces),
[Alpha Vantage daily documentation](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/) and
[private-use terms](https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/). The SEC client
identifies this project, aggregates requests at two per second, follows no
redirects and records ten-minute provider cooldowns across restart. A declared
private `stock-source-policy.json` can supply an approved request identity; no
email/credential is fabricated. No provider key or account is created.

Only official HTTPS hosts and declared SEC paths/the fixed public IBM demo are
permitted. Nonpublic DNS, credentials in URLs, alternate ports, redirects, source
oversize and slow receive fail before delivery. Sources are bounded at sixteen
MiB, chunked and verified in the existing owned G: tiers. Hot coordination stores
manifests, hashes and typed references. Cache identity retains revisions and exact
source bytes; study identity binds hypothesis/cutoff/source hashes. Reading the
saved study records information-use protection before delivery.

Reported facts retain accession/form, original value/unit, start/end, amendment,
acceptance timestamp or conservative date-only availability. Later revisions
cannot replace earlier-cutoff versions. Ratios require the same instant, USD
units and accession. Extensions/older periods are explicitly outside the bounded
six-tag/two-period projection. Current ticker maps are not historical universes.
Filing excerpts are literal bounded phrase searches, not complete section parsing
or a semantic interpretation. Source instructions are flagged and remain untrusted.

The actual documented IBM daily endpoint was read natively and archived/reopened
on an owned G: QA root. It supplied 100 daily rows, of which 99 were conservatively
available at the cutoff. Actual raw-price change was -0.0425226323119777158774373259;
SMA20 was 233.6795. These are descriptive calculations, not returns after costs,
filing causation or a point-in-time trading study. Splits/dividends, feed entitlement,
historical as-seen vintage, holiday calendar, delisting and universe coverage are
unverified and visible. Raw vendor tables remain private and uncommitted.

Native SEC access initially returned HTTP 403; the denial and persisted cooldown
are retained. The project requested the user's approved SEC request identity and
continues independent source work. Complete actual filing/section acceptance
remains blocked until permitted access succeeds. Website availability through a
different research tool does not establish this native application's access.
Optional macro/search/provider platforms were not added without a concrete
verified need. Broader entitled market acceptance remains unverified.

Fixtures cover earlier versus revised fact selection, amendments, incompatible
units/period/accessions, date-only uncertainty, missing inputs, private DNS/URL
refusal, exact multi-part archive cache/reopen, duplicate study, disclosure and
injected source excerpt. Failed amendment fixture receipts are retained; the
source amendment validator was preserved.
