# CP6 finite persistent research and capacity

The normal Experiments screen freezes a two/four/eight-iteration campaign with
four-hour untouched windows and sixty-one-minute inter-window purge. Every cycle
observes the declared boundary, diagnoses the fixed uncertainty, evaluates all
three registered numerical mechanisms, and retains or rejects the result. Coverage
is equal across families; recent winners do not determine the next hypothesis.
Reports, input/code versions, interrupted acquisitions, cancellations and rejected
proposals remain in the immutable CP4 evidence/search history. No admission or
promotion is automatic. There is no arbitrary proposal code or LLM prerequisite.

Two campaigns may be active and four are retained. FIFO experiment leases and
oldest-dispatch campaign order bound/fairly schedule work. Each iteration reserves
fifty seconds for at most two twenty-five-second child attempts, up to 400 seconds
per campaign. All windows must fit a declared expiry within 72 hours. Restart reuses
the exact linked request and declared as-of boundary. An acquisition interrupted
for thirty seconds becomes a retained failure; no later snapshot silently replaces
it. Changed numerical code cancels a frozen campaign instead of changing its method.

The sole financial writer and deterministic account engine remain separate. One
campaign may add ten or fourteen accounts; six originals are reserved, bringing
the maximum to twenty total. Four exploratory admissions also consume this cap.
Failed/stopped accounts stay retained and consume their places; there is no deletion
or top-up path to manufacture capacity. UI shows each member and whole-account
economics. Original accounts and funding are preserved.

The numerical child receives a whitelisted environment, frozen registry path and
registered evaluator, no financial DSN or provider credentials. On Windows it runs
hidden at IDLE priority on at most two allowed processors, with no GPU calls.
The supervisor polls its memory against 256 MiB and wall time against 25 seconds,
terminating and retaining an overrun. These are fixed-code capability boundaries
and supervisory limits, not an OS security sandbox for untrusted Python. Arbitrary
Python and paid/model proposals are refused. Storage/input/result/queue limits remain
CP4's 512 MiB/8 MiB/256 KiB/eight; paid and inference-call budgets are zero.

Research yields before acquisition and during a fit when paper health, capture/disk
pressure or existing workstation-resource gates are constrained. Financial position
management continues. The UI shows waiting reasons, actual attempt receipts and
conservative budget allocation; allocated wall seconds are never labeled CPU usage.
The fixed numerical alternative runs when optional advisory inference is unavailable.

Acceptance uses generated disposable databases and two real fixed child evaluations
across registry restart. This demonstrates persistence/software, not two elapsed
prospective market windows. The 1/10/20-account idle/busy benchmark predeclares four
ticks/second and thirty seconds per case. A separate CP8 finite soak extends that
contract. Repeated synthetic fits are capacity load, never additional independent
discoveries or forward profitability. No operating trial, model runtime, merge,
installation or live account is changed by this source checkpoint.
