# CP13 independent local context and flow research

`context-flow-v1` is a deterministic shadow comparison in the existing protected
registry. It never switches the installed strategy, changes an exit/size, or
admits an account. Context and flow are separate modes with separate frozen plans,
ablations and consumed windows. All failures and insufficient results remain.

The context vocabulary freezes ten-minute trend sum/strength, first/last-half
volatility ratio, developing range boundary/pullback and observed spread. Unknown
book quality is unknown liquidity; narrow visible spread does not establish deep
liquidity. Tags cannot include winning/profitable endings. Numerical retrieval
remains available independently of categories. Five outcome-blind historical event
groups retain distances, context differences and outcomes available at the query
cutoff. Context never permanently erases a numerical match. Frozen inputs retain
the complete library; exclusion counts and fixed eligibility rules reproduce all
excluded prefixes without repeating them in every report.

Flow uses at most 64 permitted snapshots over ten seconds, from one sequence-valid
streaming session, and contiguous observed trades covering the interval start.
Buyer-maker flags determine aggressive sell/buy notional; absent direction stays
unavailable. It reports visible depth imbalance, sampled top-book OFI and spread
change. Omitted updates prevent full order-flow or replenishment claims. A single
entry-wait rule requires both negative depth and aggressive pressure; baseline,
fixed-direction and component-removal controls remain distinct. Zero/three/91-second
delay sensitivity preserves expiry and cannot label late recognition as an error.

Net taken-trade labels still require CP11 reconciliation and maturity. Unknown
counterfactuals, whole-account effects, switching/turnover, exposure and marginal
monetary value remain unavailable. There is no strategy-selector/bandit adoption,
economic claim or promotion. The CP7 cost/allocation and 28-day human policy remain.

The optional D boundary is an **internal proposed packet**, not an invented
provider API. It whitelists ten returns, units, observed numerical context and five
opaque prefix references, excludes endings/ledger/private fields/instructions, and
validates finite membership, exact IDs, version and real availability/deadline.
Saved-response validation is different from a fresh provider call. The application
has no Decisions transport or credential path; access, native schema, privacy and
budget are unverified, so D supplies no additional signal and spends $0. Historical
provider contamination remains possible. Official documentation reviewed September
30: [API reference](https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/overview) and
[evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices).
These do not establish a usable Decisions wire contract for this account.

The child retains the existing two-processor/256-MiB/25-second limits and eight-MiB
inputs. Complete context/flow cohorts declare a two-MiB result cap; old modes retain
their 256-KiB cap and the registry keeps its 512-MiB physical ceiling. No evidence
is silently first-N sampled. Normal UI freezes/reopens each contribution and exposes
all saved results. See the review receipt for observed proof and limitations.
