## Retention and measured efficiency repairs

Full replay bundles remain immutable in the original 64-MiB archive. Their size
can exhaust the eight-MiB research dependency budget, especially with long account
history. A separate declared 64-MiB compact archive records the first valid prefix
per 3300-second UTC group and actual original breakout buy intents. It keeps eleven
as-seen closes, context and original committed event/journal references with hashes.
It has ceilings of 8192 prefixes and 20000 events and stops without deleting history.
An independent eight-packet/64-KiB queue continues when full replay storage stops.
The frozen memory snapshot declares its latest 512 chronological prefixes; earlier
retained prefixes are identified as outside that finite selection, not silently lost.

Net targets use the first dispatched linked buy, original closing proceeds/cost/P&L,
and a separately observed fresh 45-minute maturity. Availability is the latest of
financial commit, collection and durable write time. Fees embed once. Open trades,
late or unlinked closes and missing horizons stay unknown. These compact labels
are actual modeled ledger targets, not continuous execution replay or broker fills.
They do not establish unbiased opportunity coverage or whole-account advantage.
Old frozen plans/inputs/results and financial history are never reserialized.

Both bounded archives share one off-thread write batch. Empty compact observations
avoid redundant SQLite metadata writes. Descriptor decoding caches at most 128
unchanged text/hash pairs, each at most eight KiB; changed content cannot reuse an
old verified value. Raw/compact failure remains outside financial authority.
The runtime passes scalar book features and actual feature availability to the
prefix builder; dataclass books and future bar arrivals cannot become fake context.

The UI now shows learning comparisons as a readable table, names contributions by
purpose, catches corrupted pending-request JSON, exposes compact capture state and
preserves frozen review retries. The existing reference dashboard/accounts/risk
layout from Chris's three inspiration images remains intact.
