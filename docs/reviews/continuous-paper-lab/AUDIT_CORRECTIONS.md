## Corrections

1. **Maintenance continuation.** The v2 index acknowledges each verified temporary
   reclamation. A bounded pass moves past absent sources after an interrupted unlink;
   it verifies the retained checksum before acknowledging them and never invents a
   reclaimed-byte count. Protection and unlink share the index's write transaction.
2. **Full evidence acquisition.** A bounded read-only adapter acquires v1/v2 decision
   dependencies in chronology, deduplicates identical packet hashes and keeps typed
   exact references. Frozen intervals refuse missing, changed, unavailable or oversized
   dependencies. Retained gzip segments and temporary SQLite segments both work.
   Order-flow, account-memory, exit, sizing and observation-priority consumers use it;
   the normal exact-capture panel can request an isolated replay. First-seen capture
   metadata is durable through a lost index acknowledgment. Older v2 records without
   that metadata remain unavailable for causal interval evaluation; their original
   bytes/references remain readable. No current timestamp is backfilled into history.
3. **Queue progress.** Permanent parent/policy invalidity is retained as a rejected
   proposal. Temporary data/allocation waits retain a reason and per-job retry time;
   other eligible work can proceed. Blocked jobs count against the existing bounded
   queue. Financial reservation and funding checks remain authoritative.
4. **Information boundaries.** Bundle issuance registers disclosed outcome intervals
   in the same SQLite transaction/authority used for untouched evaluation admission,
   before returning the bundle. Concurrent requests serialize; retries are idempotent.
   Prior disclosures may be reused for training, not new holdout confirmation. Available
   continued compact outcomes participate, with source/outcome hashes checked.
5. **Coherent feature sampling.** The numerical feature accepts only declared one,
   five or fifteen minute spacing. The strategy passes its frozen sampling interval.
   Original minute defaults, complete warmup, gap/future/stale guards and cost hurdles
   remain. Actual range features produce engine order intents at all three horizons.
6. **Durable horizon allocation.** An independent successful reservation advances a
   journaled cursor exactly once in the sole financial transaction. Bounded result
   retrieval does not determine rotation. Retry/rejection/restart do not advance or
   reset the cursor; existing child and trial windows stay frozen.

The original eight accepted requests and their order remain authoritative. These
corrections amend PR24's implementation and proof; they do not replace the installed
investigation, charts, complete lab lifecycle or their unchanged safety requirements.
