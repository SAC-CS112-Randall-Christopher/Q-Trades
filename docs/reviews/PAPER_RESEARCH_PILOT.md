# Experimental paper research pilot — goal #66

Chris authorized connecting the tested trained-v2 model to the existing research
worker on October 6, 2026. Its mission is to discover useful improvements through
evidence-driven experiments and learning while the independent paper execution
engine manages admitted strategies. Experiment quality, falsification, retained
outcomes and justified next questions matter more than response speed or a quota
of model calls.

The pilot is experimental and **unqualified**. Its explicit paper research grant
does not fabricate a role qualification, strategy edge, model promotion, external
review approval or a completed qualification window. It does not authorize live
trading, paid requests, RAG, new training, model downloads/conversion or holdouts.

## Useful experiments and limits

The model receives frozen causal numerical/market inputs and the existing reviewed
capability catalog. The current supported methods include replication of the
reviewed reference, an independent reviewed alternative and bounded variations
with an eligible parent trial. Supported reviewed components can be selected only
when their original artifact identity and current eligibility are satisfied.
This catalog does not permit arbitrary generated executable code, an invented
strategy family or automatic strategy promotion.

An honest `request_data`, `no_change`, negative result or inconclusive outcome is
useful when its evidence and next dependency are retained. New closed bars or a
mature matched paper outcome can justify a later distinct question. Missing input
does not justify regenerating a preferred answer, treating a synthetic fixture as
market evidence or counting calls as research quality. The normal usefulness and
cost report continues to disclose measured usage, unknown counts, missing matched
comparison coverage and selection limitations.

## Normal workflow and recovery

Open **AI Lab → Local model research** to inspect the experimental status, exact
profile, current protected admission, actual owned task and worker recovery reason.
The existing research-question form, retained history and task details remain the
normal path. A saved question is distinct from an executed inference. Reopening a
task shows its actual attempts, answers, failures and supported next dependency.
Pilot model review is labeled as model review; it is not represented as qualified
external review. Pilot attempts do not expose a training export control under this
authorization.

**Pause paper research** delegates to the existing pilot owner, stops new requests
and cancels its owned active request without deleting questions or paper history.
**Resume approved paper pilot** preserves the prior grant/profile and rechecks
current protected admission. It cannot create a grant, reverse a revocation or
promote a model. Control requests require the normal local operator and same-origin
checks. An unknown control acknowledgment remains retained across reload; fresh
status can reconcile the intended state only when it exposes a validated policy
and the matching configured grant state. An unavailable, malformed or revoked
policy does not count as a successful pause acknowledgment. Otherwise the operator
explicitly retries the same idempotent control. Definitive refusals remain visible
and allow correction.
