# A second implemented hypothesis after a saved pattern comparison

The preceding pattern policy can use a mature lesson, but its only executable
comparison is p0. After that candidate is used, a later question has no new
implemented mechanism to propose. This successor adds one independent comparison
through the existing experiment and financial owners. It makes no claim that the
new mechanism is profitable or better than the original.

## Exact source policy

The optional `qtrades-peft-paper-pilot-v8` format requires the existing
`reviewed-rule-role-v8` answer contract and the explicit
`bounded-pattern-method-question-v1` policy. It additionally requires
`method_policy_sha256`, binding the following declaration:

| --- | --- | --- |
| p0 | breakout-retest-v1 | cost-breakout-v1 |
| p1 | trend-pullback-v1 | cost-breakout-v1 |

Both use the already implemented v4 mechanisms, medium horizon and unchanged
cost, risk, hold and review controls. The two fixed comparisons share a reference;
they are different hypotheses, not a refinement of a parent financial trial.
This route remains scoped to a supported saved BTCUSD/native-5m finding and its
separately retained current minute inputs. Other timeframe coverage is context;
the daily five-market shortlist does not make every market executable here.
A p1 proposal must have `parent_trial=None`. The unchanged output grammar,
prompt, profile, model resource limits and transport do not confer operating
authority for this new policy.

The worker selects an unused declared method with the existing capacity and
protected input checks. It binds the method ID and policy hash at selection and
checks them through preparation, current-input observation, publication, answer,
review, inbox and recovery. Legacy p0 wire and default intent remain
unchanged. Saved p1 evidence names its actual method and cannot offer a control
that silently prepares or observes p0 instead.

## What the previous outcome means

An own-authority mature p0 outcome can inform p1 at a distinct later native event.
The retained evidence identifies the original method separately from the next
method. It preserves the original outcome, lesson, costs, adverse or incomplete
facts, source coverage and unknown causes. A historical detector finding is
motivation; it is not equivalent to the executable bank's entry decision.

If p0 was already used under a different grant, this new grant may consider unused
p1 only with an explicit absence of its own prior lesson. It cannot adopt that
foreign grant's task, attempt or outcome. If both declared candidates are used,
the policy cannot invent another candidate or duplicate a used financial pair.
Read-only study of retained evidence does not supply fresh funding authority.

Known role-training disclosures are retained and explicitly distinguished from
unknown protected, qualification or prospective intervals. The original default
p0 overlap behavior is unchanged. Incomplete or adverse outcomes are neither
overwritten nor retried for a preferred answer.

Only the new policy's model projection compacts repeated metadata. Matched byte
measurements describe this reduction; they are not a model admission limit.
Actual PEFT admission uses the runner's complete tokenized prompt and answer
reserve against the approved profile. The original detector explanation is referenced by its
SHA rather than sent as literal text; kind, time, level, volume and five-frame
coverage remain explicit. Exact original preparation, lesson and outcome sources
remain retained and reopenable. Original scored financial fields, samples, types
and nulls remain lossless. This distinction must remain visible in packet proof;
it is not a claim that every original explanatory field is supplied literally or
that the model has successfully interpreted the projection.

When a follow-up includes both outcomes, the prior outcome may reference exactly
equal fields in the same packet's current outcome. Differing fields and removals
are explicit; the prior outcome keeps its own original source identity and SHA.
Reconstruction checks compare each complete original scored body, including
sample fields, types and nulls. This avoids repeated fields without combining
the two financial outcomes or using a reference outside the packet.

## Separate operating decision

This successor is a source proposal. It creates no operating profile/grant file,
installs nothing and starts no operating research, acquisition or model call.
The accepted c653 installation and its documented ownership and workflow limits
are reused. Earlier finite-v6 and daily-history approval requests remain separate;
neither transfers to this new policy or changes the original attempt permission.

Operating use requires review and approval of the exact successor stack and
merged SHA, supported updater handoff, exact existing model/profile and new
method-bound grant selection, and a defined prospective paper/model test scope
with request, time and comparison limits and retained outcomes. Existing guards,
sole financial writer, twenty-slot capacity and 400/100-GB policy remain protected.
Acquisition, promotion, training, holdouts, paid calls and other operating changes
retain their applicable separate authority.
