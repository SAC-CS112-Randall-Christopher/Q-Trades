# CP7 protected prospective comparisons and learning journal

The normal Paper screen creates an explicit separately funded frozen incumbent
control for a numerical challenger, inspects/retains a complete-account comparison,
exports its reasons/evidence, and shows drift and recorded signal decisions. The
control starts with the same capital, instrument universe, execution assumptions,
operating allocation and hard-stop policy. It consumes a remaining place in the
twenty-account cap; admission cannot discard or replenish another account. The
simple-exposure benchmark now supports a declared BTC-only numerical universe;
original BTC/ETH benchmark records and default allocation remain unchanged.

Policy prospective-paper-role-v1 is frozen before forward comparisons: 28 complete
subsequent daily blocks, no arbitrary trade quota, matched eligible windows and
known operating costs, positive incumbent/cash/exposure advantage and a doubled
execution-cost proxy plus additional slippage, ten-percent within-window drawdown
limit, at least 75% positive daily incumbent excess, and no exceptional day making
up more than half of positive excess. Mean excess must exceed four descriptive
Newey-West errors with seven daily lags. This is a conservative paper-role rule,
not a p-value, calibrated independence or proof of an economic edge. All correlated
families, parameters, failed trials and earlier inspections remain selection history.

Comparisons read at most 512 completed financial-journal windows with explicit
truncation refusal. Windows before admission/control freeze or the protected
information boundary cannot become new validation. Inspection itself extends that
boundary even when the result rejects or subsequent persistence fails. Reports
retain original event IDs, complete liquidation-account returns (including cash,
owned holdings, fees and funding treatment), costs, controls, excluded-window
reasons, daily blocks, uncertainties and artifact/configuration hashes. Negative,
synthetic, unmatched, drifted and insufficient results are explicit no-promotion.

An eligible report still requires explicit local-operator approval for a **paper
research incumbent role**. This designation points to an already funded forward
account; it does not transfer funds, rewrite its rules or replace the running
original primary trial. The prior account continues with its own losses/policy.
Approval is fenced by report/configuration hash, fresh worker/risk health, expiry,
compared incumbent and role version. Retries are idempotent. Tested rollback changes
the role back while all accounts, journals and reports remain inspectable. There
are at most 32 retained reports and eight role designations, without history pruning.

Data/holding-mark gaps, invalid artifacts, version mismatches, cost changes and
latched loss/processing failures are drift/attention states. There is no silent
retraining or loss reset. Instrument/exposure and actual execution/operating costs
are reported. Regime attribution stays unknown; no causal story is invented. The
doubled-cost calculation is a conservative accounting proxy, not historical replay
with changed fill opportunities. Numerical incumbents copy their validated frozen
artifact into the separately funded control; its new admission boundary remains
binding and no retraining occurs.

The UI distinctly labels frozen strategy-role decisions, retained research memory,
separate reviewed code, unchanged prompts and no AI-model training. Synthetic tests
exercise a constructed qualifying contract and rollback mechanics; this is not
28 days of observed returns. The actual QA workflow correctly retains no-promotion.
Subsequent real elapsed forward evidence is still required. No operating trial,
live order/funding/key permission, paid call, merge or installation is changed here.
